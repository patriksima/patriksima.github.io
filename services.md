## Who This Is For

I help companies building or operating systems on **Azure and .NET** when they face at least one of these challenges:

- systems don't talk to each other — integrations are brittle, manual, or missing,
- you want AI in your product or processes, but need proof before committing,
- a migration, refactoring, or architectural change feels too risky to start,
- there is no strong senior technical leadership for priorities and execution.

The goal is simple: **connected systems, less risk, faster delivery**.
