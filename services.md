## Who This Is For

I help companies building or operating **.NET and Azure** systems when they face at least one of these challenges:

- the application is slow, costly, or unreliable,
- the team needs guidance for a risky technical change,
- there is no strong senior technical leadership for priorities and execution.

The goal is simple: **less risk, better performance, faster delivery**.
