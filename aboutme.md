## How It All Started

My journey into tech began in childhood at a church school where they had a PMD computer. I have no idea how I ended up in front of it, but I did. Then my grandfather bought me a [**Didactic M**](https://cs.wikipedia.org/wiki/Didaktik_M) – I still remember reading the Slovak manual (probably my first Slovak book ever).

I taught myself **assembly language** and tried creating games, but lacked the knowledge back then. When the Pentium PC era arrived in '95, I dove into low-level stuff – experimenting with viruses, learning C, getting into Novell NetWare. The atmosphere of the **Invex exhibitions in Brno** captivated me completely – the internet, BBS boards, the hacker, cracker, and demoscene culture. I've been hooked ever since.
